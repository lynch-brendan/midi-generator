# TENSjr

Free studio spring reverb plugin by Klanghelm, a stripped-down version of TENS, based on the well known AKG BX high end studio spring reverbs from the 60s through to the 80s.

## Presets on disk
Not applicable — this plugin exposes presets via the standard VST/AU program API.

## Notable parameters
- **Decay** — reverb time in seconds; a single tank is stereo and the range of decay times has been extended to 0.5 to 20 seconds.
- **Modulation** — introduces movement and reduces ringing in the reverb tail.
- **Character** (horizontal fader) — provides a range of options from splashy through to dark and moody.
- **Input mode** — mono or stereo input to the reverb tank.
- **Dry/Wet mix**

## Quirks
- Free "jr." version of TENS: features only TENS model 5 - tank A; lacks the full TENS Metallic/Whoosh/Tension controls, amp/preamp saturation section, modulation matrix, and envelope follower.
- Sounds almost like a plate reverb due to its "very high reverb density, fast build-up and very low 'boing' artifacts." The developer contrasts this sound with that of the classic spring reverbs in guitar amps, organs, and synths.
- Available for windows and macOS as a VST, AU and AAX plugin, Apple Silicon compatible, no iLok necessary.
- Current version 1.0.7.
