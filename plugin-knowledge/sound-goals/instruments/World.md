# World Instruments

Ethnic and world instruments — sitar, koto, banjo, kalimba, bagpipe, shanai, and other non-Western voices. Reach for these when the user asks for a specific world instrument or a genre that uses one (Bollywood, Celtic, African, East Asian).

## Options

### GM Sitar (program 104)

- **Character:** Indian plucked-string instrument, drone-heavy
- **Best for:** Indian classical, Bollywood, psychedelic rock, world-fusion
- **Emotional tags:** exotic, meditative, hypnotic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="sitar", channel_name="Sitar", gm_program=104)`

### GM Banjo (program 105)

- **Character:** twangy, plucked, American folk instrument
- **Best for:** bluegrass, country, folk, indie-folk
- **Emotional tags:** rustic, cheerful, nostalgic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="banjo", channel_name="Banjo", gm_program=105)`

### GM Shamisen (program 106)

- **Character:** Japanese plucked-string instrument, sharp attack
- **Best for:** Japanese traditional, anime/game scoring, world-fusion
- **Emotional tags:** exotic, sharp, dramatic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="shamisen", channel_name="Shamisen", gm_program=106)`

### GM Koto (program 107)

- **Character:** Japanese zither, delicate plucked strings
- **Best for:** Japanese traditional, meditative scoring, ambient
- **Emotional tags:** delicate, contemplative, exotic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="koto", channel_name="Koto", gm_program=107)`

### GM Kalimba (program 108)

- **Best for:** African music, world-fusion, lo-fi, chillhop
- **Emotional tags:** playful, meditative, warm
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="kalimba", channel_name="Kalimba", gm_program=108)`

### GM Bagpipe (program 109)

- **Character:** Scottish/Celtic double-reed with drone
- **Best for:** Celtic, folk, cinematic Scottish scoring

- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="bagpipe", channel_name="Bagpipe", gm_program=109)`

### GM Fiddle (program 110)

- **Character:** folk violin — brighter and more agile than orchestral violin
- **Best for:** Irish/Celtic, bluegrass, Appalachian folk
- **Emotional tags:** cheerful, energetic, folky
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="fiddle", channel_name="Fiddle", gm_program=110)`

### GM Shanai (program 111)

- **Character:** Indian double-reed instrument, nasal and reedy
- **Best for:** Indian classical, Bollywood, world-fusion
- **Emotional tags:** exotic, ceremonial, plaintive
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="shanai", channel_name="Shanai", gm_program=111)`

### GM Ocarina (program 79)

- **Character:** small clay flute, soft and airy
- **Best for:** Andean folk, game-music (Zelda), fantasy scoring

### GM Pan Flute (program 75)

- **Character:** breathy flute made of tubes, South American folk
- **Best for:** world music, cinematic Andes scoring, ambient
- **Emotional tags:** breathy, mysterious, exotic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="panflute", channel_name="Pan Flute", gm_program=75)`

## Comparisons

- **Sitar vs Shamisen vs Koto:** sitar is Indian/drone-heavy; shamisen is Japanese/sharp-attack; koto is Japanese/delicate.
- **Kalimba vs Banjo:** kalimba is African/mellow; banjo is American/twangy.
- **Fiddle vs GM Violin:** fiddle is brighter and folkier; violin is more orchestral.

## What we're missing
