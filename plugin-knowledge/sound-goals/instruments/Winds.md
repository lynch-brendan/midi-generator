# Winds

Woodwinds and brass — flute, oboe, clarinet, sax, trumpet, trombone, tuba, French horn. Reach for these when the user asks for a real acoustic wind or brass voice.

## Options

### GM Flute (program 73)

- **Character:** breathy, airy, top-register wind
- **Best for:** melodies over pads, folk/celtic, cinematic top lines, jazz solos
- **Emotional tags:** delicate, dreamy, pastoral, playful
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="flute", channel_name="Flute", gm_program=73)`

### GM Oboe (program 68)

- **Character:** nasal, penetrating, distinctive double-reed voice
- **Best for:** melancholic solo lines, orchestral color, folk
- **Emotional tags:** plaintive, lonely, expressive, exotic
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="oboe", channel_name="Oboe", gm_program=68)`

### GM Clarinet (program 71)

- **Character:** warm woody midrange, smooth, agile
- **Best for:** jazz solos, klezmer, orchestral color, ballads
- **Emotional tags:** smooth, mellow, thoughtful, wistful
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="clarinet", channel_name="Clarinet", gm_program=71)`

### GM Alto Sax (program 65)

- **Character:** smooth, vocal-like, mid-register sax
- **Best for:** jazz solos, pop hooks, R&B, smooth ballads
- **Emotional tags:** sultry, smooth, warm, expressive
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="sax", channel_name="Alto Sax", gm_program=65)`

- **Best for:** jazz, rock, blues, soul, punchy solos
- **Emotional tags:** confident, warm, powerful, cool

### GM Trumpet (program 56)

### GM Piccolo (program 72)

- **Character:** ultra-high flute, piercing, brilliant
- **Best for:** marches, orchestral highlights, cinematic sparkle
- **Emotional tags:** bright, celebratory, piercing
- **How to use:** `load_gm_instrument(channel_id="piccolo", channel_name="Piccolo", gm_program=72)`

## Comparisons

- **Flute vs Piccolo:** flute is the singing melodic voice; piccolo is one octave up, for accents or marches.
- **Alto vs Tenor sax:** alto is smoother/mid-range; tenor is warmer/lower and more powerful.
- **Trumpet vs Trombone:** trumpet cuts on top; trombone provides brassy mid.

## What we're missing

Ensemble brass patches (SBS Brass Section, staccato brass hits, muted trumpets). Everything is via GM — good for demos, not for produced film music.
